Progress for big files.
- While a file goes out you see the percent and the size, and you can pause, continue or cancel it.
- On Android the progress sits in the main notification list, with the buttons there and on the home screen.
- On iPhone the upload keeps going when you close the share sheet or leave the app.
- On iPhone you can send a file from the app with the paperclip button.
- After a pause the file is sent again from the start.
